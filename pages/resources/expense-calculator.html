Detailed Breakdown of Monthly Expenses in Dubai

1. Accommodation Options in Dubai (Living Type)

Housing typically consumes 30-50% of monthly income in Dubai. Our calculator includes these options:

  • Bed Space (1,000 - 1,200 AED): The most budget-friendly option where you share a room with 4-6 people in bunk beds. Common in labor accommodations and budget shared apartments. Ideal for those prioritizing savings over privacy. Includes shared bathroom and kitchen facilities.
  • Partition (1,500 - 1,800 AED): A semi-private solution where large rooms and halls are divided into 3-4 sections using temporary walls. Offers more privacy than bed space while remaining affordable. Popular in areas like Deira, Bur Dubai, Al-Barsha, Al-Rigga and Al Nahda.
  • Sharing Room (2,000 - 2,300 AED): A room shared with just 2-3 people where each occupant has their own bed and a cupboard. Common in family apartments and buildings converted to shared housing. Provides reasonable privacy at a mid-range price point.
  • Personal Room (3,000 - 3,500 AED): Your own private room in a shared apartment. The premium option for those who value privacy and personal space. Prices vary significantly by location - expect higher rates in Dubai Marina, Dubai Internet City (DIC), Jumeirah Lakes Tower (JLT), Jumeirah Village Triangle (JVT), Jumeirah Village Circle (JVC), Al Sufouh or Downtown compared to International City or Discovery Gardens.

2. Mobile Plans in Dubai

Telecommunication costs can vary based on your usage patterns:

  • Normal (100 AED): Basic prepaid plan with 2-4GB data and limited minutes. Suitable for those who primarily use WhatsApp, LinkedIn (+ other social media channels) and WiFi. Providers like Du and Etisalat offer competitive starter packages.
  • Active (150 AED): Mid-range plan with 8-12GB data and more flexibility for local + international minutes. Ideal for professionals who need reliable connectivity for work communications and moderate social media use.
  • Extensive (250 AED): Premium postpaid plans with 15-20GB data, unlimited minutes, and more international call benefits. Recommended for heavy users who connect, communicate and stream content regularly or make frequent international calls.

3. Food Costs in Dubai

Your eating habits significantly impact your monthly budget:

  • Cooking at Home (500 AED): The most economical option. Monthly groceries for one person from supermarkets like Carrefour or Lulu Hypermarket. Includes basic staples, some fresh produce, and occasional treats. Requires time for meal preparation but saves significantly compared to eating out.
  • Eating Outside (800 AED): For those preferring convenience. Includes daily meals at economical cafeterias (in areas such as Al-Barsha, Burjuman, Sharaf DG, Al Quoz, Al-Nahda, Mankhool, Deira and Al-Rigga) or affordable restaurants. Breakfast (20 AED), lunch (25 AED), and dinner (30 AED) at mid-range eateries.
  • Partially Both (600 AED): Balanced approach with home-cooked meals 60-70% of the time and occasional dining out. Breakfast at home, packed lunch, and eating out 2-3 times weekly at reasonably priced restaurants.

4. Transportation via Dubai Metro

Dubai's public transport is efficient and cost-effective:

  • Normal (100-150 AED): Occasional metro use (15-20 trips monthly). Ideal for those who live near their workplace or use a combination of walking and occasional metro rides. A single trip costs 3-8.50 AED depending on zones.
  • Active (200-250 AED): Regular commuter (20-25 workdays monthly). Includes daily work commute plus some weekend travel. Monthly Nol cards offer better value for frequent riders with additional savings compared to single tickets.
  • Extensive (250-300 AED): Heavy metro user (30+ trips monthly). For those who extensively use public transport for work, socializing, and exploration across multiple zones. Consider the unlimited monthly pass (30 days at just 350 AED) if your usage exceeds 40 trips or beyond your planned trips for a month.

5. Social Life Expenses

Networking and leisure activities impact your budget:

  • Just Coffee Meetups (150-300 AED): Basic socializing at affordable cafes (15-20 AED per outing). Includes 2-3 weekly meetups at places like Tim Hortons or local Arabic cafes. Minimal entertainment expenses.
  • As and When Required (200-400 AED): Moderate social budget. Includes occasional dinners (50-80 AED) at mid-range restaurants, plus some entertainment activities like movie tickets (50 AED) or museum visits.
  • Attending Social Events (300-500 AED): Active social calendar with weekly outings. Includes brunches (60-90 AED), club entries (50-100 AED with drinks), and regular participation in networking events or hobby groups.

  • Emergency funds should be separate from your monthly budget
